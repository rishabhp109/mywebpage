About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at The Chinese University of Hong Kong Shenzhen, working in the area of Theoretical Chemistry. My research focuses on Non-adiabatic dynamics, Vibronic Coupling, and Light-Matter Interactions.

I received my Ph.D. in Theoretical Chemistry from Indian Institute of Technology Patna, India, where I worked with Prof. Tamminneni Rajagopala Rao. My broader research interests include interdisciplinary approaches to understanding complex scientific, technical, or societal problems.
