About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at The Chinese University of Hong Kong Shenzhen, working in the area of Theoretical Chemistry. My research focuses on Non-adiabatic dynamics, Vibronic Coupling, and Light-Matter Interactions.

I received my Ph.D. in Theoretical Chemistry from Indian Institute of Technology Patna, India, where I worked with Prof. Tamminneni Rajagopala Rao. My broader research interests include quantum nuclear dynamics and its applications in reproducing the photo-electron spectrum through ab initio electronic structure calculations and quantum nuclear dynamics for different poly-atomic molecular systems. Solved the Hamiltonians for various polyatomic systems and examined the non-adiabatic effects present in them through potential energy curves and the nuclear dynamics studies.

Research Interests

Vibronic Coupling

Theoretical Elucidation of vibronic coupling in the Photoelectron Spectra of polyatomic molecular systems.

Electronic Structure Calculations

Obtaining the various physical and chemical properties of compounds via DFT calculations.

MCTDH

Multi Configurational Time-dependent Hartree algorithm utilised to solve vibronic coupling problems via TD and TI quantum chemistry methods.
